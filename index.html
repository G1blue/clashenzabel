Simplified axisymmetric anterior-segment shell (not patient-specific). Cornea/limbus are modeled here as the Yeoh-material region and sclera as the Neo-Hookean region, matching the material split used in the FEA methodology. Aqueous humor is modeled as a filled fluid volume bounded by the inner cornea surface and an independent iris/lens boundary, closed at the anterior chamber angle. Units are millimeters, same as the microstent geometry tool, so parts line up directly.
